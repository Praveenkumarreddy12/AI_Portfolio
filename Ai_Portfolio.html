About

I am an AI engineer with two years of experience building production multi-agent and retrieval systems. My focus is turning LLM workflows into dependable services: specialised agents with clear roles, retrieval grounded in real evidence, and an asynchronous backend that keeps up with live data.

The problems I work on involve many scattered sources, such as logs, metrics, deployment history and past incidents, that need to be pulled together into an answer a person can verify. I design outputs to carry their evidence, confidence and next steps, and I track prompts and evaluations with MLflow so changes can be compared.

I am interested in Generative AI, Agentic AI, LLM application and Python backend roles.
